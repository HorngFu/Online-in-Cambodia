# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Online in Cambodia to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, language, or political view.

## Our standards

**Do:**

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers and to people writing in their second language.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the accuracy of the directory.
- Respect the privacy of individuals and organizations.

**Don't:**

- Use insulting, discriminatory, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Harass anyone, publicly or privately.
- Publish anyone's private information (phone numbers, home addresses, personal accounts) without permission.
- Use the directory to promote, attack, or defame businesses, organizations, or political groups.
- Add spam, fake listings, or misleading links.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, the content editor, and community chats) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. **Correction:** a private note explaining the problem.
2. **Warning:** a formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** from project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban** for repeated or severe violations.
